# Physics-journal response practice

Use this reference to turn an established scientific answer into a complete, professional response for a physics journal. It does not establish the truth, novelty, or importance of the scientific claims.

## Establish authority and coverage

Apply the current sources in this order:

1. the decision letter and editor instructions;
2. the exact referee comment;
3. the current manuscript and controlling evidence;
4. the target journal's current response and revision guidance.

Copy or identify each independent part of a referee comment and answer it point by point. A concise response may combine parts only when one scientific answer genuinely resolves them together. If the user's intended scope leaves out a point that the editor or journal requires authors to address, expose that conflict instead of silently expanding the response or pretending coverage is complete.

## Answer from the scientific record

- When no manuscript change is warranted, give the evidence-backed reason rather than defending the work in general terms.
- Treat a referee's misunderstanding as a signal to inspect the original reading path, not as automatic proof of either a writing failure or a referee error.
- Use professional, collegial language without canned thanks, repeated deference, or rhetorical escalation. Accuracy and complete coverage carry the respect.

When required by the journal, provide exact page, section, figure, or line locations and both clean and marked manuscript versions. Additional scientific support needed to sustain the response belongs in the manuscript or another authorized submission artifact, not only in private correspondence.

## Sources and boundary

This practice follows the American Physical Society's guidance on [responding to referee reports](https://journals.aps.org/authors/responding-to-referee-reports) and [Physical Review web submissions](https://journals.aps.org/authors/web-submission-guidelines-physical-review), together with IOP Publishing's guidance on [preparing a revised article](https://publishingsupport.iopscience.iop.org/questions/how-to-prepare-your-revised-article/). Recheck the target journal's current instructions when preparing an actual submission.

General online writing skills may suggest useful response structures, but they are design samples rather than authorities for physics-journal procedure. Do not import fixed sentence limits, mandatory gratitude formulas, or the premise that every referee misunderstanding proves a writing defect.
